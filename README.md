## About

The **Language and Vision Laboratory (LV-Lab)**, formerly the *Learning and Vision Lab*, is a research group at the [School of Computing, National University of Singapore](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/), led by [**Prof. Shuicheng Yan**](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/yansc/) (Fellow of SAEng, AAAI, ACM, IEEE, IAPR; H-index > 150).

We focus on advancing the foundations of artificial intelligence through the integrated study of vision, language, robotics and other modalities. Our work lies at the intersection of multimodal learning, large-scale model optimization, and cognitive-level decision-making, with the goal of bridging low-level sensory input and high-level intelligence.

## Research: Towards e-AGI

Our long-term vision is **e-AGI** — building AI that is **Efficient**, **Executive**, and **Evolving**.
